Inside the boundary

Exception rules, priority queue, ownership and escalation, evidence-backed outcomes, audit log, data-freshness guard, outcome measurement, English and Vietnamese UI.

Outside, read only

Order and warehouse systems keep owning orders, stock and dispatch. PromiseOps never writes back; if it is down, staff use the current floor process.

Deliberately not in the MVP

  • Write-back, routing, replenishment, promise changes
  • Customer messaging: "handed to customer service" is recorded, not sent
  • AI in the product (rules only)

One cycle, every 5 minutes

1 the adapter reads each live feed and records that the read succeeded · 2 duplicates are ignored, bad records rejected and logged · 3 if the warehouse feed is older than 30 min, or was not read after a milestone, absence-based alerts pause and a data task opens · 4 otherwise the 2 rules create one exception per order and cause, owned by the coordinator, with a 15-minute response timer · 5 a person acts; expedite needs the shift lead; every outcome needs evidence · 6 every change lands in the audit log · 7 outcomes are measured against the original promise.
